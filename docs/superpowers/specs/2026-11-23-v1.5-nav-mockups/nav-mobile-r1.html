Both: 44 px touch targets, the Previous/Next bar stays at the bottom, the sheet closes on any tile tap, Escape, or the ✕. The sheet is a proper dialog for screen readers. My pick: M1 — students flip between lesson and sandbox often, and a hidden mode causes “where did my lesson go?” confusion.
